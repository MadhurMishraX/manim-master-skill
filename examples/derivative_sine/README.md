# Derivative of Sine

## Goal

Show that the derivative of `sin(x)` is `cos(x)` by connecting a moving point on the unit circle to the slope of the sine graph.

## Scenes

### Scene 1 - Unit circle

Introduce a point moving on the unit circle.

### Scene 2 - Slope idea

Show the sine height and connect it to the graph.

### Scene 3 - Takeaway

Show the final formula:

```txt
d/dx sin(x) = cos(x)
```
